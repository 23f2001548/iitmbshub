# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19 + Vite 8 + TailwindCSS 4

## Users

Prospective students browsing resources to understand the IIT Madras BS Degree program, alongside current students looking for quick access to tools and portals.

## Product Purpose

An all-in-one companion hub that centralizes scattered resources, official portals, tools (like Grade Predictor), and community links for the IITM BS Degree. Success means a student never has to hunt for a link again.

## Positioning

The only unofficial, student-built unified directory that cuts through the noise of scattered official emails and forums, presenting everything in one fast, definitive list.

## Operating Context

Students use this hub while multitasking, often under stress (checking grades, finding assignment links right before deadlines), or prospective students exploring the ecosystem casually. Speed and clarity are paramount.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Needs to link to various external tools and internal pages (Grade Predictor, Calendar).
- Fully responsive on mobile (where many students check scores).

## Brand Commitments

- "Made with ❤️ for the IITM BS Degree Community"
- Open source, student-built, un-affiliated officially but highly useful.
